An amalgamation, not a pivot

Dan Ebanks (Daniel Ebanks) is the Founding Account Executive at Anytime AI in Toronto. The career behind that title is an amalgamation of skills built over the years, not a pivot.

  1. 2012–2018

    University of Toronto

    Honours BSc in Biology and Chemistry.

  2. 2019–2021

    Taro Pharmaceuticals

    Quality control chemist.

  3. 2021–2025

    PatSnap

    Senior Global Account Executive in life sciences.

  4. April 2025–present

    Anytime AI

    Founding Account Executive, ranked No. 1 in revenue in several months of 2025.

The chemist's curiosity started with planning and running a chemical reaction: set a hypothesis, control the variables, observe, and adjust. He brings that into every sales conversation. People have told him he is too nice for sales. A Jamaican-Canadian upbringing is why. Jamaicans make people feel seen, and Canadians are courteous. Together, they make a warm approach that is the opposite of the stereotypical salesperson.
